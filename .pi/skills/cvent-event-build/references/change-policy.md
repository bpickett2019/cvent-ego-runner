# Policy authority

[SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) is the only client business-policy authority. Do not copy its
rules into technical references or revive rules from historical jobs or other skills.

Business approval does not add executable capabilities. The [runner integration](runner-integration.md),
[browser controls](browser/controls.md) and [discount tool](discounts.md) describe the
supported transports and their limits. A tool denial is not permission to switch
transport, change ownership, delete a marker or replay an operation.

Use the [source guide](rr-interpretation.md) to retain original and policy-derived
values separately. Use the [result contract](runner-contract.md) to record what was
actually saved and what remains unfinished. Neither presentation nor a policy change
rewrites historical evidence or verifies Cvent configuration.
